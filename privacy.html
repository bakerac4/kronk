Privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026.

Kronk is a private household assistant used only by members of one household.

What it accesses

Kronk uses Google sign-in only to read Google Calendar events from calendars that household members have explicitly shared with its own Google account. Each person can stop sharing a calendar at any time from Google Calendar's settings.

How data is used and stored

  • Calendar data is used only to answer household members' questions, prepare their daily summaries, and send their reminders.
  • All processing happens on a single computer in the household. Calendar data is not sent to any outside AI service or other third party.
  • Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Kronk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by unsharing the calendar.
